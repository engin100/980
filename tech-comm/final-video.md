## Purpose

The purpose of this assignment is to give you experience translating the technical work you’ve done this semester into a professional, investor-focused video. Doing this type of multimodal “restructuring” of already existing information is useful in and of itself; however, focusing it toward a new audience provides additional rhetorical opportunities. You will gain experience brainstorming ideas, creating a script, and creating and editing a video. All of this will be in service of communicating persuasively and effectively.

## Task

Remember the focus you picked for your Project Review? This assignment asks you to build upon that by creating a “Kickstarter video” that proposes a company, a product/products, a system/systems, a professional organization, or any other capital-focused idea based on your project’s focus.

Whatever your proposal is, you will want to tell the story of your work this semester. Did you face setbacks or challenges? Talk about what these things taught you and how your team is now better prepared for success with your proposed idea.

Your video will include the following elements:

- Your proposed product, company, etc.

  - Think of this as the introduction to your video

- Sufficient technical detail

  - Reference your final report frequently, e.g. “... more information about thrust profiles can be found in section II of our final report.”

  - Tech side fill in here

- Discussion of what you will do with investor funding

  - Think of this as the next steps of bringing your pitch to fruition. Will you need to develop a company? Conduct market research? Develop some sort of engineering infrastructure?

- Discussion of possible societal, cultural, and/or environmental impacts

  - This is where you make your proposal more persuasive by including things that are not strictly business-focused

- Strong conclusion that offers key takeaways

- Pictures and/or videos of your work

- No longer than 5 minutes

- Serious in nature

  - While you can use humor as part of your video (in fact, humor is often a valuable rhetorical tool\!), your video should be professional

- Include captions

## Rhetorical Details

Don’t assume your audience knows your reasoning. Remember that your goal is to convince your audience to give you capital. Simply presenting data without guiding your audience through your interpretations is not enough.

## Criteria for Success

This team assignment is worth 20 technical communications points and 5 technical points. Technical Communication elements of the video will be assessed on the following criteria:

- Argument. Video makes a well-reasoned business proposal based on work you’ve done this semester. All claims are supported by evidence and clearly explained.

- Organization. The video makes reasonable choices for how to organize information for an audience of investors. It follows a logical progression of sections and information within discreet video sections.

- Preparation. Video shows sufficient work and preparation from team. Your video does not have to be well produced, but it should be clear that you have put time and effort into making it.

- Formal Elements. All elements of the video are proofread and free of grammatical and/or formatting errors. Includes References and acknowledgements at end of video.
