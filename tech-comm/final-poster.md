## Purpose

Posters are a common genre within STEM. Despite their ubiquity, they are often poorly designed and do not effectively communicate information. This assignment asks you to use the \#betterposter framework to create a poster that does effectively communicate information.

## Task

Remember the focus you picked for your Project Review? This assignment asks you to build upon that by creating a poster that highlights your proposed business idea and presents a holistic analysis of both technical and business elements.

Use the \#betterposter framework to make a poster that translates the work you’ve done this semester, and the data your payload has gathered, into a business proposal. It must include

- Title that centers proposal

- Holistic proposal that combines technical information with business, environmental, civil, ethical, and/or cultural considerations

- Strong information scent that balances informative function with lowering audience cognitive load. Your poster should be easy to follow without verbal guidance

- Clean and well designed visuals

- References on poster and on QR code linked Google Doc

## Criteria for Success

This team assignment is worth 20 technical communications points and 10 technical points.

Technical Communication elements of the video will be assessed on the following criteria:

- Visual impact and clarity: Poster has a strong “information scent” and balances informative function with cognitive load considerations. It’s aesthetically pleasing, well organized, and easy to follow without needing verbal guidance.

- Verbal delivery: The presentation of the poster is clear, engaging, and shows evidence of preparation and familiarity with the material without being scripted or memorized.

- Sociotechnical analysis: The poster & presentation are centered on a holistic analysis that combines technical factors with business considerations.

- Formatting & Finishing: Poster demonstrates professionalism in writing, visuals, and design and has been checked for errors.
