## Purpose

You have experience creating multiple slide decks by this point in the semester. You will build on that experience by creating a final slide deck that takes this work and translates it to a professional, investor-focused slide deck.

## Task

Remember the focus you picked for your Project Review? This assignment asks you to build upon that by creating a slide deck that functions as an informative and persuasive aide to the rest of your final deliverables.

You will want to outline your proposed product, company, professional organization, etc., before going into detail on how it functions and how you propose to generate revenue from it.

Your slide deck can and should be a more advanced version of your Project Review and Go/No-Go decks. Think of these as rough drafts of your final slide deck and edit the work you’ve already done into something persuasive and investor focused.

Your slide deck will include the following elements:

- Introduction to your proposed idea

  - Team members

  - Sufficient technical detail

- Level 1 requirements translated to new situation

  - What requirements do you have for your proposed idea? How will your sensor board fulfill them?

- Visuals

  - System level block diagram, budgets and testing plots (to show proof of concept for proposed idea), sensor board physical specifications

  - What else?

- Business elements

  - How much funding do you require, what will you use funding for, pricing/cost structure of product/company, how long do you predict until you can generate revenue, and so on

- Discussion of possible societal, cultural, and/or environmental impacts

  - You can likely pick business-focused elements of your video, report, and poster and include them to satisfy this requirement

- References

  - On slide and Reference page

## Criteria for Success

This team assignment is worth 15 technical communications points and 5 technical points.

- Argument. Slide deck makes a well-reasoned business proposal based on work you’ve done this semester. All claims are supported by evidence and clearly explained.

- Organization. Slide deck proceeds logically from broad to detailed. Slide deck has appropriate sections. Slides use assertion-evidence form in place of topic sentences. Slides focus on single main idea.

- Visuals. Visuals follow best practices discussed in class for slide decks, the type of visual meets the needs communication purpose, formatting elements (such as axes, titles, callouts, legends) are carefully selected, visuals are clean and aesthetically pleasing

- Formal Elements. All elements of the packet are proofread and free of grammatical and/or formatting errors. Includes in-text (on slide) and Reference page in IEEE.
